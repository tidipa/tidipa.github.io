## Summary

At Sāvatthī, in Jeta's Grove, the bhikkhū sitting in the Kareri pavilion fall to talking about former lives, and the Bhagavā joins them. He recounts the six Buddhas before him and himself: the aeon of each, his birth, clan and lifespan, his tree of awakening, his two chief disciples, his assemblies, his attendant and his parents. He then tells the life of Vipassī in full: the wonders of his birth and the thirty-two marks, the four sights and his going forth, his insight into dependent origination, Brahmā's request, his first disciples and the sending out of his bhikkhū. The gods of the Pure Abodes confirm the account to him.

## Commentary

- The sutta tells the Buddha's own story as Vipassī's: the account of the `bodhisatta` from birth to the sending out of the disciples follows the Mahākhandhaka's account of Gotama step by step, with Brahmā's request and the going-forth formulas word for word the same. The Khandhaka pages are therefore its twins on this site: [Brahmayācanakathā](/khandhaka/1-5/), [Bodhikathā](/khandhaka/1-1/).
- The seven Buddhas' particulars in part 1 are repeated by the gods of the Pure Abodes in part 10, in the same sentences.
